# Weekday devotion roster and provisional minutes

The county administrative assistant can generate one roster for each county and year in the Monday workspace. The server uses randomized rounds of currently active, confirmed county staff: ordinary assignments differ by at most one at creation time, with an attempt to avoid consecutive leaders across rounds. This is not a guarantee of fairness after staff leave, join, or assignments are changed. The roster is a snapshot, not a continuously rebuilt schedule. A roster generated partway through the year includes past dates for planning consistency, but those entries do not prove that a devotion or presentation actually happened. Assistants can change individual leaders with a recorded reason before minutes exist; such changes may affect fairness. A generated year cannot be silently overwritten.

The provisional special days are Ash Wednesday, Good Friday, Easter Monday and Christmas Day, when they fall on weekdays. These are assumptions pending the organization's calendar. They are reserved for the Country Director. If there is not exactly one active, confirmed Country Director, they remain unassigned until the assistant selects an eligible director. Only Tuesday–Thursday are shown as 08:30–09:00; Monday and Friday times are explicitly unspecified. The week display covers year boundaries, and a full-year list is available.

The assigned leader can prepare a Bible reference, prayer/verse notes, discussion and actions ahead of a meeting (including Sunday or Monday) and explicitly share the record. Drafts are visible only to the leader and the county assistant. Shared minutes are visible to scoped county staff, and to permitted organization-wide staff; closing prayer can be recorded by the leader or county assistant. A server-validated version and audit event accompany each action. Shared minutes are locked against silent rewriting; an amendment workflow has not yet been built. Avoid patient details in these staff minutes. Leader substitution after minutes exist is not supported in this version.

Seven Bible Gateway links (NIV, KJV, NKJV, ESV, NLT, NASB and CSB) are provided for the recorded reference. No translation text is stored or redistributed; the provider controls availability and licensing. Links require internet and send the entered reference to the external provider. Do not put personal information in a Bible reference.

The minutes fields and the special-day list are provisional, not a substitute for the official organizational template or calendar. Synthetic tests cover rotation, authorization, private drafts, scoped full and delta reads, explicit sharing, closing prayer, adjustment audit, and version conflicts. Real staff acceptance, a browser/device test and production data/account verification remain separate tasks.
