# Planning and PDCA work in progress

- Weekly Planning Indicators default to the last completed Monday–Friday work week in Africa/Nairobi. Dated events use that period; standing totals remain current register figures, not historical snapshots. The displayed **provisional** 52-week rule begins week 1 one Monday before the Monday of the week containing January 1. Later segments beyond week 52 are labelled additional week 52 segments, each with its actual dates. This matches the supplied example of 16–20 November 2026 as week 48, but is not the organization's confirmed rule. To avoid collisions and avoid relabelling historical reports, archive identifiers still use the existing provisional ISO week key; displayed week numbers and archive identifiers can therefore differ. Replace this convention only after the official rule is supplied and migration is designed.
- Confirmed paper receipt, randomly drawn ministry-area ordering and secretary-controlled progression are implemented as server-backed, scoped and audited actions; clinical-date reference rows remain separate. Late additions after the draw require a reviewed process outside this version. See `MONDAY-WEEKLY-REVIEW.md` for the provisional meeting minutes and limitations.
- An MCM can explicitly authorize sharing a reviewed under-120 PDCA or a 120+ PDCA that has completed its existing correction and authorization chain. The assigned caregiver or MCM can then share the immutable authorized revision. International clinical users can initiate line-anchored comment, question, clarification and evidence-request threads, with replies from international reviewers, scoped MCMs and the assigned caregiver. Commands enforce revision anchors, authorization and optimistic version checks; the discussions are advisory and do not silently modify the clinical report.
- PDCA printable HTML/PDF and Word have the foundation letterhead and logo. The shared Word writer embeds the logo in DOCX exports; Word layouts and all other report types have not received complete bespoke visual redesign or real-application print acceptance.
- The weekday devotion roster, provisional special-day assumptions, minutes, closing prayer and external translation links are described in `DEVOTIONS-AND-ROSTER.md`. The organization's exact observances and actual templates remain to be supplied. Shared devotion minutes cannot yet be amended, and an existing roster is not dynamically rebalanced after staff changes.
- Secure hosted offline login and refresh after loss of network remain blocked as described in `OFFLINE_SECURITY_REQUIREMENTS.md`; the shell alone is not offline clinical access. No claim of full offline synchronization, safety or data retention is made. The managed-device provider is still undecided.
- Synthetic tests and build checks cannot establish the actual deployment's patient count, real-account login behavior or physical printer behavior. Real browser/device acceptance remains outstanding.
