# Foundation programme scope

Owner clarification: the Foundation does not provide household schooling assistance. School fees, uniforms, books, keeping children in school and school-continuity outcomes must not be presented as care-programme services, goals or achievements.

Care content should describe medical support, treatment adherence, nutrition, guardian/family care and associated support already established in the programme. Health education/counselling is different from schooling assistance and remains valid. Clinical demographic education history, age bands and safeguarding restrictions on identifying children or their schools do not imply school support and are not erased by this clarification.

This change corrects public descriptions, programme imagery, example care goals and seeded narratives. It does not rewrite existing live clinical histories or remove safeguarding protections. Previously saved data may contain older wording; review it through authorised record-correction procedures rather than silently rewriting patient records.
