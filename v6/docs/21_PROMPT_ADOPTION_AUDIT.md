## Part E · The v6.2 increment (this branch)

1. **The rename** — the product is LOMAMO C-DIMS everywhere it is read: app identity, bundle title, site entry page, credential master file, assistant prompt, README and guide. `APP.formerName` keeps "CareLink DIMS" so older printed reports and the offline kits still resolve.
2. **`household.js`** — households and cross-account care chains (Part A item 4c).
3. **`waiting_urgent.js`** — the Waiting List and URGCAS registers, with promotion into an assessment and urgent-case costs feeding the cost engine.
4. **`master_analysis.js`** — the workbook's analysis layer: rows 152–160 per area, both band schemes, the slum/village ratio, cost per visited patient, the multi-year series, the configurable programme year, and the source-integrity register (285 `#REF!` cells, 11 dead defined names, the Homa Bay external link, the comment threads, the template variants, the empty Goals sheet, and the verified absence of macros).
5. **Analytics** — a `areaClass` pivot dimension (slum or village) and a "came from the waiting list" dimension, plus two panels: the live ratio against the workbook baseline, and visited patients by year.
6. **Cost engine** — the Foodbags week × ministry area matrix on the Monday-to-Saturday week rule, and urgent-case cost lines.
7. **Two robustness fixes found by the suites** — a shared *guardian arrangement* ("Both parents") no longer links half the register together, only a named guardian does; and the demonstration seed is anchored to midnight, because anchoring it at 09:00 made every programme-day count one day short when the suite ran before nine in the morning.
