## 4. Honest Statement of Environment Boundaries & Untested External Dependencies

In strict compliance with `PROMPT 1 §1.1.14` and `PROMPT 2 §16`:
1. **External Live KHIS / DHIS2 / FHIR / Laboratory Servers:** CareLink generates standards-conformant DHIS2 `dataValueSets` JSON, HL7 FHIR R4 `Bundle` payloads, and internal MOH 731 / MOH 711 verification drafts, and explicitly marks every output **not an official KHIS submission**. Live transmission to Ministry of Health KHIS servers requires official MOH credentials and M&E sign-off.
2. **Physical Multi-Device Wi-Fi & Hardware Printing:** Multi-client concurrent patching, firewall rule generation, and LAN binding (`0.0.0.0`) are automated and verified in automated HTTP tests; physical multi-tablet Wi-Fi walk-testing and physical paper printer layout checks take place on the host Windows PC in Kisumu.
3. **Lovable.ai Static Preview vs Local LAN Host:** When published on Lovable.ai for international partner review, the full interactive C-DIMS v6 application runs in client-persisted mode inside the Vite/React shell; shared multi-device office SQLite hosting (`server.py`) runs on the LOMAMO office host computer.
