## 1. The master patient workbook

`20260101 LOMAMO registration patients 2026.xlsx` was read sheet by sheet:
21 sheets, 145 columns on each ministry-area register, live formulas, the `Tabel`
validation lists, the 53-week Monday-to-Saturday calendar, the weekly foodbag
grid, the quarterly hygiene-pack columns and the cost columns EA to EL.

Everything learned from it is now data in the application:

* **`MASTER_COLUMNS`** — the column map, grouped as the workbook groups it
  (Identity, Programme days, Household & children, Print & schedule, Weekly bag
  grid, Bag totals, Hygiene packs, Medication & TB, Grants & conditions, Faith &
  support items, Hospital, Cost), each with its printed label, its validation
  list, its formula as written, and the CareLink field it becomes.
* **`MASTER_VOCAB`** — every dropdown the workbook offers: months, weekdays,
  schedule codes (`EW`, `EOW`, `1 Month`), bag codes (`BG1`, `BG2`, `SPB`),
  cover (`NHIF`, `LMM`, `Other`), cancer sites, ministry areas and the 2026 week
  calendar.
* **`MASTER_RULES`** — the workbook's arithmetic rebuilt as code and tested:
  days in programme `=IF(I="";0;J-I)`, the minimal-care flag `=IF(AND(L="";K>183);"x";"")`,
  total children `=P+Q`, orphans prevented `=IF(L="";"";R)`, became orphans
  `=IF(M="";"";R)`, bag counts `=COUNTIF(AB:CB;Tabel!$I$5..7)`, total other cost
  `=SUM(EB:EE)`, in-patient cost `=SUM(EG:EJ)`, total `=EF+EK`, costprice food
  per visited patient `=C/D`, the slum/village ratio, the closing control and the
  long-stay share.
* **`MASTER_INDEX_MAP`** — all 24 report topics listed on the workbook's own
  INDEX sheet, each resolved to a page that exists in the system (the suite fails
  if one does not).
* **`MASTERX.mapRow()`** — turns a workbook row into a patient record, its
  conditions, its support issues and its cost lines, carrying the source sheet
  and row for audit.
* **Registration** — the new-patient form now carries the master registry fields
  (cover, spouse's ID, male and female children, schedule, bag code, repeat
  beneficiary, relocated, medical only, TB type, cancer site, ZvAfrika grant and
  the five cost columns), and the derived measures are calculated on save rather
  than typed.
* **A console** — *System → Master registry mapping* prints the whole map, the
  blueprint, the worked formulas and the personal-data decision.

**Personal data.** The workbook holds patient names, National IDs, telephone
numbers and household detail. This repository is public and serves the live site,
so the file was studied, mapped and then removed from it
(`git rm "20260101 LOMAMO registration patients 2026 (1).xlsx"`). What is kept is
the structure, the vocabulary, the rules and the mapping — no patient identity.
Real records are loaded through *System → Master data import* on the office host,
using the CSV template the mapping console downloads.

> The file still exists in the repository's Git history: removing it from the
> working tree stops the live site serving it, but a blob in history stays
> reachable at the commit that introduced it. Erasing it needs a history rewrite
> plus a GitHub support request to clear cached views. That is prepared in
> `v6/tools/purge_patient_data.sh` with the runbook at `v6/docs/22_PURGE_RUNBOOK.md`,
> and was not executed unilaterally because it rewrites the branch every
> deployment depends on. The commit is not named here on purpose: a published
> SHA plus a path is a signpost, and this document is public.

## 2. Authority: level, domain and context

The reported violation was real: the M&E / Data Officer held
`report.targets.manage` and could therefore set a target for a county office.
Measurement and target setting are now separate duties, and every capability that
writes targets, money, patient data, drugs or organisational information is bound
to three things at once — a **level** (area → county → national), a **domain**
and a **context** (the county, area, department or record involved).

* New capabilities: `report.targets.view|area|county|national`,
  `cost.view|ledger|standard.manage|standard.national|export`,
  `tb.view|request.create|request.review|dispense|stock.count|stock.reconcile`,
  `team.view|team.manage`.
* `report.targets.manage` was withdrawn from `me`, `auditor` and `intldata`. They
  keep `report.targets.view`: they read targets, report against them and raise a
  data-quality exception. They cannot choose one.
* `CAP_DEPT` binds a capability to the departments that own the subject matter,
  so Finance cannot confirm a diagnosis, Media cannot pay an invoice, and a
  caregiver cannot adjust pharmacy stock.
* `CAP_LEVEL` binds a capability to the deepest level it may reach; the context
  decides whether this particular act is inside it. A County Office Administrator
  cannot set a national target; a coordinator cannot propose a target for another
  area.
* Enforcement sits in `RBAC.can` — the same single gate the data layer already
  used — wrapped by `access_rules.js`, so views, form handlers, workflow
  transitions and the repository all meet the same test, and every refusal is
  audited with the rule that caused it.
* *Assurance → Access & authority audit* prints the whole binding as a matrix and
  runs a live self-test of thirteen authority rules.

## 4 & 5. Cost per patient

`costing.js` attributes every cost the system already holds — financial
transactions, support issues, costs registered on a home-visit or hospital-visit
form, hospital-request transport fares, and TB dispensing — to a patient, then
rolls it up ten ways: category, ministry area, county, national, month,
department, bag type, disease, per patient and per patient-day.

* **Two questions, one engine.** A caregiver, coordinator, case manager, nurse or
  hospital focal person asks *"what did it cost to serve my patients?"* and sees
  the whole cost of the patients in their scope. Pharmacy, hospital coordination,
  fleet and procurement ask *"what did my service cost?"* and see their own
  service. Finance, Audit and national reach see everything inside their
  geography, including the raw ledger.
* **Confidentiality.** Disease-level cost needs clinical aggregate authority.
  Without it the engine shows cost buckets by category group and says why.
* **Unit cost standards** turn an issued item into money where no receipt exists
  yet (Bag 1, Bag 2, Special bag, hygiene pack, Water Guard, pyridoxine courses,
  transport trip, outpatient visit, in-patient day). Receipts always win. Finance,
  Procurement and the County Office Administrator set county standards; only the
  Director, President or Chief Program Administrator sets a national one. Every
  change is audited.
* **Reports.** *Finance & procurement → Cost per patient* has ten tabs, a
  printable report with aligned numeric columns, and a CSV export that is audited.
  The patient record carries a cost panel; the ministry area console carries the
  area total and average.
* **Charts.** `charts.js` gained line, area, stacked, grouped, donut, share bar,
  gauge, heat grid, radar, sparkline and waterfall charts — all inline SVG, all
  theme-aware, all carrying a written summary and a data table beneath.

## 6. TB dispensing control

`tb_dispensing.js` implements the protocol exactly as it was given:

1. A caregiver requests pyridoxine for a TB patient from the ministry console
   (10-day standard, two-week, or one-month with a written clinical reason).
2. The Medical Case Manager answers — approve, adjust or refuse — and feedback is
   mandatory on an adjustment or a refusal. The caregiver is notified with the
   feedback text.
3. A course given today covers ten days, so the next window is **day 9 to day 10**,
   calculated from the dispensing record and never typed. A patient past the
   window is flagged overdue; past the grace period, a possible missed dose.
4. Dispensing takes stock from a named batch, earliest expiry first, writes the
   movement to the stock ledger and records the cost for the cost engine.
5. A physical stock count is recorded, compared with *procured − dispensed +
   adjustments*, and confirmed by Finance or Procurement. A variance outside
   tolerance (the greater of 5 tablets or 2%) opens a finding and notifies
   Finance, Procurement, Audit and the County Office Administrator. Confirming can
   write the ledger to match the shelf, with the reason kept on the line.
6. *Clinical & pharmacy → TB dispensing control* has six tabs: control board,
   requests & feedback, dispensing schedule, stock & counts, procurement &
   reconciliation (Finance/Procurement/Audit), and the protocol with the dose
   arithmetic worked day by day.

## 7 & 9. The visual and accessibility audit

`audit6.css` is the correction layer, and `visual_audit.js` is the instrument that
proves it. Four classes of defect were found and fixed:

* **Hard-coded light surfaces.** Panels written as `#fff` before themes existed
  stayed white in a dark theme while their text followed the theme, so the text
  vanished: message threads and bubbles, the assistant panel, the modal header and
  footer, stage blocks, chips, key caps, hover states, status pills and the mobile
  card form of a table. All re-pointed at theme tokens.
* **Legibility on coloured fills.** The jewel-gradient metric boxes set white text
  on the box but the label and the detail line kept `var(--muted)`: dark grey on
  deep blue, unreadable in every light theme. Every child of a filled metric box
  now carries a light ink. Tabs, count pills, segmented controls, badges and
  buttons were checked the same way, and links inside tinted panels use
  `--accent-fg` (solved against the tint) and are underlined, so they are never
  colour alone — this is the map's "Clear selection" control.
* **The fixed console bar.** It held eleven controls in one non-wrapping flex row
  and had **two assistant buttons**, so on a tablet the live pill, the sync chip,
  the search box and the account name collided. It is now a measured five-region
  grid (logo · history · identity · search · actions) with one assistant button —
  labelled on wide screens, icon-only below 1240px — and a defined collapse order
  at 1240, 900 and 560 px.
* **Alignment and symmetry.** Header cells were padded 14px and body cells 12px,
  so columns did not line up. One padding rhythm now, tabular figures everywhere a
  number appears, right-aligned numeric and action columns, sticky headers, banded
  rows, and a shared report-table measure so a printed report lines up with the
  screen.

Also in this release: the message **people list scrolls** inside its own box with a
sticky heading; the assistant's welcome card, both bubble directions, the
suggestion chips and the input row follow the theme (a sent message used the ink
token as its fill, which in a dark theme was white text on a white bubble); and a
**tablet opens in Harbour Dusk**, because a personal choice wins, then an explicit
organisation default, then the device.

`visual_audit.js` carries the published specification of the field device the
programme uses — Samsung Galaxy A11 class (SM-A115): 6.4" PLS TFT, 720 × 1560 HD+,
19.5:9, 268 ppi, 60 Hz, Snapdragon 450, 4000 mAh, 15 W, 161.4 × 76.3 × 8.0 mm,
177 g, Android 10 → 12 (One UI Core 4.1) — and corrects the earlier documentation,
which quoted 720 × 1560 as CSS pixels; at dpr 2 the CSS viewport is **360 × 780**.
The audit console measures every theme's contrast pairs, runs a live layout audit
(collisions, clipping, touch targets, header-to-cell alignment, page overflow),
inventories hard-coded colours still in the sheets, and lists what this release
fixed and how each fix is proved.

## 10. First-run setup could not get past step 3 of 4

The cause was found and it is not a mystery. On step 3 the client posts the
first-run document to the office host. If the host already holds an installation
it answers `409 already_setup` — correctly, because first-run setup must never
overwrite a live host — and the client printed that answer as a red error and
**stayed on step 3**, with no way forward.

Both sides were fixed:

* `v6/server.py` now answers with the reason *and* what the person needs: how many
  accounts exist, which senior offices may issue one, and the three options. No
  credential, hash or personal record is returned.
* `v6/src/entry6.js` treats the answer as an answer. Step 3 becomes a recovery
  panel with three honest ways forward — **sign in with the account you were
  issued**, **ask the administrator for an account**, or **continue on this device
  only** — and the primary button stops re-posting setup. The wizard can no longer
  strand anyone.

The panel also explains the case the user was probably in: if you *are* the
administrator and need to commission that host again, restore a backup or clear
the host database first (Backup & restore on the host), then run setup again.

## 11. What is deliberately not done here

1. **Git history.** The master workbook is removed from the working tree but still
   exists in the repository's history, and is therefore still reachable at the
   commit that introduced it. Find it locally with
   `git log --all --oneline -- "20260101 LOMAMO registration patients 2026 (1).xlsx"`
   and erase it with `v6/tools/purge_patient_data.sh` (runbook: `v6/docs/22_PURGE_RUNBOOK.md`). Erasing it needs `git filter-repo` (or BFG) across
   the branch that GitHub Pages serves, a force-push, and a GitHub support request
   to clear cached views and forks. Say the word and it will be done as its own
   change, because it rewrites the history every deployment depends on.
2. **A shared central database.** The hosted build still stores its data in each
   visitor's browser unless the office host (`v6/server.py`) is running on the
   office Wi-Fi. That is the offline design of this system, not an oversight; the
   hosted site is a demonstration and a delivery channel. For staff to share one
   set of records online, run the host on the office PC, or move the repository
   layer onto a server database (see `v6/docs/01_ARCHITECTURE.md` §4).
3. **Homa Bay field days** and the **Nyalenda visit day** are marked provisional
   and wait on Administration.
4. **Seats Administration has not named** (medical case managers, clinical
   officers, finance, M&E, HR, audit, ICT, homestay, some caregivers) keep a seat
   label and an "awaiting confirmation" flag rather than an invented person. They
   are listed in *People → Ministry teams & visit days* and on the staff register.
